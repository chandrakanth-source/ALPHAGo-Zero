AlphaGo Zero Arena

A scaled-down Python/PyTorch replication of DeepMind's AlphaGo Zero (Silver et al. 2017) with live MCTS visualization & REST inference backend.

Model Card

  • Architecture: Dual-head ResNet (3 Residual Blocks)
  • Input Planes: 3 (Current, Opponent, Empty)
  • Board Sizes: 12x12 & 5x5 Kaya Boards
  • Scoring: Territory Rules with Komi offset

Hosting Architecture

  • Frontend: Hosted on Vercel static CDN
  • Backend Engine: FastAPI on Render free tier
  • Health Check: /health endpoint

RULES & GUIDE

How to Play Go

1. Board & Territory

Black moves first. Players alternate placing stones on line intersections. The goal is to surround empty intersections (territory) and capture opponent stones.

2. Captures & Liberties

Stones of the same color connected along lines form a group. A group is captured and removed when all its adjacent empty points (liberties) are occupied by enemy stones.

3. Passing & Game End

If you have no profitable move, click Pass. When both players pass consecutively, the match ends and total territory + stones are counted.

4. Komi & MCTS Engine

White receives bonus points (Komi: 7.5 on 12x12, 0.5 on 5x5) to offset Black's first-move advantage. Higher MCTS simulations increase AI tactical strength.
